01 Scan

Captured to 0.02 mm.

We scan your car, part or panel with a metrology-grade blue laser scanner. It's accurate to as little as 0.02 mm, thinner than a human hair, and takes up to 2.8 million measurements every second.

  • 0.02 mmaccuracy
  • 2.8Mpoints per second
  • Blue laserhandles dark and shiny paint
02 Process

Cleaned up for you.

Raw scans are messy. We fill the gaps, remove noise and stray points, align everything and merge it into a single clean, watertight mesh, so you never have to touch the raw data.

03 Deliver

An STL ready to design on.

You get a ready-to-use .STL file that opens in the CAD software your designer or manufacturer already uses. They can start designing parts, kits and accessories around your car straight away.

  • Autodesk Fusion
  • SolidWorks
  • Autodesk Inventor
  • Siemens NX
  • CATIA
  • Onshape
  • Rhino
  • Blender
